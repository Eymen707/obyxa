Free. Version 1.1. Needs Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer.

How to install

  1. Download the file above.
  2. Open it. Minecraft starts and imports the add-on by itself.
  3. Edit your world, open Behavior Packs and activate “TNT + Car + Furniture Mod”. The resource pack comes with it.
  4. Create or load the world and open the Creative inventory.

If your browser saves the file as .zip, rename it to TNT_Mod.mcaddon. If blocks do not show up, turn on “Holiday Creator Features” under Experiments in the world settings.

What’s inside

TNT blocks
5x, 20x, 25x, 30x and 50x TNT. Light them with flint and steel or a fire charge. A nearby explosion sets off the next one. Find them under Items.
TNT Bazooka
Right-click to fire a rocket that explodes on impact. Sneak and right-click to switch between the five TNT powers. Find it under Equipment.
Cars
Bugatti Chiron and Car. Use the item on the ground to place the car, right-click it to get in, drive with the movement keys and steer with your view. Sneak to get out.
Furniture
Chair, table, sofa, floor lamp, TV and wardrobe. Find them under Construction.
Textures
New textures for every block and item, plus a fresh look for vanilla TNT.
