GAMERWOST TNT Craft

Place TNT, light it and blow up the world. Everyone who opens this page joins the same lobby automatically.

Connecting…

Move with A/D or the arrow keys and jump with W or Space. Left click uses the selected item, right click mines. Keys 1–9 pick an item. On a phone use the on-screen buttons and tap the world. To play with friends, open the same page. If you all type the same lobby name, you land in the same game.

Download the TNT Mod for Minecraft Bedrock

Free add-on with TNT up to 50x, a TNT Bazooka, a drivable Bugatti Chiron, a car and furniture.

Download TNT_Mod.mcaddon

Version 1.1. Needs Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer. If your browser saves the file as .zip, rename it to TNT_Mod.mcaddon.

What’s inside

TNT Bazooka
Right-click to fire a rocket that explodes on impact. Sneak and right-click to switch between the five TNT powers.
Cars
Bugatti Chiron and Car. Use the item on the ground to place the car, right-click to get in and sneak to get out.
Furniture
Chair, table, sofa, floor lamp, TV and wardrobe.
Textures
New textures for every block and item, plus a fresh look for vanilla TNT.

How to install

  1. Download the file and open it. Minecraft imports the add-on by itself.
  2. Edit your world, open Behavior Packs and activate “TNT + Car + Furniture Mod”.
  3. Load the world and open the Creative inventory. TNT and cars are under Items, furniture under Construction, the bazooka under Equipment.

If the blocks do not show up, turn on “Holiday Creator Features” under Experiments in the world settings.
