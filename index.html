How to Install

  1. Download the file above.
  2. Open it. Minecraft will automatically import the add-on.
  3. Edit your world, open Behavior Packs and activate "TNT + Car + Furniture Mod". The resource pack comes with it.
  4. Create or load your world and open the Creative inventory.

If your browser saves the file as .zip, rename it to TNT_Mod.mcaddon. If blocks do not appear, enable "Holiday Creator Features" under Experiments in world settings.

What's Inside

TNT Blocks
5x, 20x, 25x, 30x and 50x TNT. Light them with flint and steel or a fire charge. Nearby explosions can trigger other TNT blocks. Find them under Items.
TNT Bazooka
Right-click to fire an explosive rocket. Sneak and right-click to switch between the five TNT powers. Find it under Equipment.
Cars
Bugatti Chiron and Car. Use the item on the ground to place the car, right-click to get in, drive with movement keys and steer with your view. Sneak to exit.
Furniture
Chair, table, sofa, floor lamp, TV and wardrobe. Find them under Construction.
Textures
New textures for every block and item, plus a fresh look for vanilla TNT.
